The screenshot shows the project opened correctly (the "App" project and its CapApp-SPM package loaded, iPhone 17 Pro Max selected). The middle pane says "No Selection" because the project settings aren't opened yet — that's the next step.

## Step 1 — Open the project settings
- In the **left sidebar**, click the blue **App** icon at the very top (next to the word "App" with the arrow)

## Step 2 — Set your signing team
- In the middle panel, select the **Signing & Capabilities** tab
- Under **Team**, click the dropdown and choose your Apple account (Team ID 2DLWZ7MYZ5)
- If no team appears yet: Xcode menu → **Settings → Accounts → + → Apple ID** and sign in with your paid developer account, then return here and pick it

## Step 3 — Run the app
- Make sure the device menu at the top still shows an iPhone (e.g. iPhone 17 Pro Max)
- Press the **▶ Play** button (top-left, next to the square stop button)
- The first build takes a few minutes — watch the progress at the top of the Xcode window

## What success looks like
A simulator window opens showing the Wooffy app's landing page. If you see a **red error** instead — especially anything mentioning "signing", "team", or "bundle identifier" — paste or screenshot it and I'll fix it with you.
