# iOS App Store Submission — Walkthrough & Package

## The big picture (read this first)

Your app is a website wrapped in a native iOS shell (Capacitor). Apple never sees your web code directly — they review a native iOS project built on your Mac in Xcode. The process has 4 phases:

```text
1. PREPARE (here, with me)
   I generate the native iOS project folder + icons/splash + checklist

2. BUILD (on your Mac)
   Open the project in Xcode, set your Apple Team, build

3. UPLOAD (Xcode → App Store Connect)
   Archive and upload the build to your Apple Developer account

4. SUBMIT (App Store Connect website)
   Screenshots, description, review info → Submit for Review
```

## What I will deliver

A downloadable folder containing everything iOS needs:

1. **The native iOS project** (`ios/` folder) — generated by running `npx cap add ios` + `npx cap sync` against your latest web build, so it matches the current published app (not the July version).
2. **App icon & splash assets** — generated from your Wooffy logo at every size Apple requires (including the mandatory 1024x1024 App Store icon).
3. **A step-by-step submission guide** (plain-English PDF/markdown) covering every click from opening Xcode to pressing "Submit for Review", including:
   - Xcode signing setup with your Team ID (2DLWZ7MYZ5)

   - The privacy nutrition-label answers for your app (account data, etc.)
   - What to write in "Review Notes" for Apple (demo account if needed)

## What you will do (the walkthrough)

**Phase 1 — On your Mac (about 30 min):**
1. Download the folder I give you, unzip it
2. Install Xcode from the App Store (free, large download) if not installed
3. Open `ios/App/App.xcworkspace` in Xcode
4. In Signing & Capabilities: select your Team, confirm Bundle ID `app.wooffy`
5. Plug in an iPhone or pick a simulator, press Play to test

**Phase 2 — Upload (about 15 min):**
6. In Xcode: Product → Archive
7. When the archive finishes: Distribute App → App Store Connect → Upload
8. Wait ~10-30 min for Apple to process the build

**Phase 3 — App Store Connect listing (about 1 hour):**
9. Go to appstoreconnect.apple.com → My Apps → New App
10. Fill in: name (Wooffy), subtitle, description, keywords, screenshots (I'll give you the exact sizes and can generate them), privacy policy URL (wooffy.app/privacy or /terms), support URL
11. Answer the age-rating questionnaire and privacy nutrition labels (my guide has the exact answers)
12. Select the uploaded build, then Submit for Review

**Phase 4 — Review:**
- Apple reviews in ~24-48 hours typically. They may ask questions; my guide includes likely questions and answers (e.g. why the app needs an account, how memberships work — note: your memberships are real-world services, so no Apple In-App Purchase is required).
